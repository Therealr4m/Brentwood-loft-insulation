Expert loft boarding and insulation for Brentwood residents

Many homeowners across Brentwood want to make better use of their loft space, but simply laying boards directly over insulation can cause problems. When insulation becomes compressed, it cannot perform properly and heat can escape more easily.

Our loft boarding and insulation fitting service creates a practical storage area while protecting the insulation underneath. We install a raised boarding system that allows insulation to maintain the correct depth instead of being flattened.

This gives you a safer, more usable loft while helping your home remain warmer and more energy efficient.

How our loft boarding and insulation installation works

1. Loft assessment

We inspect your existing insulation, loft layout and available space before starting. This allows us to understand whether your current insulation can stay in place or needs improving before boarding.

2. Raised boarding installation

We install a suitable support system above the insulation layer, preventing compression while creating a stable surface for storage boards.

3. Finished usable loft space

Once complete, your loft has a safer storage area while your insulation continues working effectively to reduce heat loss.

The problem with crushing loft insulation

A common issue we find in Brentwood homes is insulation that has been compressed because boards have been fitted directly onto the loft joists.

Although the loft may appear usable, compressed insulation loses some of its ability to trap heat. This can reduce comfort levels and make your heating work harder.

A correctly installed raised loft boarding system avoids this problem by creating separation between the storage platform and the insulation below.

  • Protects existing insulation performance
  • Creates a safer storage area
  • Helps prevent cold spots caused by reduced insulation depth
  • Allows ventilation to be maintained correctly

Loft boarding and insulation questions Brentwood homeowners ask

Can I board straight over my existing loft insulation?

It is not recommended. Boarding directly over insulation can compress it and reduce its effectiveness. A raised boarding system keeps the insulation at the correct depth while still creating storage space.

How much does loft boarding and insulation installation cost in Brentwood?

The cost depends on your loft size, access, existing insulation condition and the amount of boarding required. We provide a clear quote after assessing your loft space.

How long does loft boarding installation take?

Most standard loft boarding installations can be completed within a day. Larger lofts or projects requiring insulation upgrades may take longer depending on the work involved.

Will loft boarding make my loft suitable as a room?

No. Loft boarding creates a safe storage area only. It is designed for accessing stored belongings, not converting the loft into living accommodation.

Do I need new insulation before loft boarding?

Not always. We assess the insulation already in place and advise whether it can remain, needs topping up, or should be improved before boarding.
